# Introduction

The **Customer Service Microservice** is a comprehensive Customer Relationship Management (CRM) system designed for the QaliTrack platform. This service manages customer data, contact information, contracts, and order processing within the weighbridge operations ecosystem.

### Key Technologies:
- **Backend:** ASP.NET Core 8.0 (C#)
- **Database:** SQLite with Entity Framework Core
- **Architecture:** Clean Architecture (API, Core, Infrastructure layers)
- **Authentication:** JWT integration-ready
- **API Documentation:** Swagger/OpenAPI for interactive documentation and testing

The API includes endpoints for:
- Customer profile and data management
- Contact person management for customers
- Contract creation and management between customers and organization
- Order processing and tracking
- Integration with weighbridge operations for customer-specific transactions

### Business Context:
The Customer Service acts as the central hub for customer relationship management in the QaliTrack weighbridge platform, enabling:

- **Customer Onboarding**: Streamlined registration and profile management
- **Contract Management**: Digital contract creation and lifecycle management
- **Order Processing**: Efficient order creation and tracking
- **Contact Management**: Maintain relationships with key customer personnel
- **Integration Ready**: Seamlessly integrates with other QaliTrack services like transaction processing and reporting
